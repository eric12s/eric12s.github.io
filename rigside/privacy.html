Rigside – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Rigside is an unofficial companion app for the Neural DSP Nano Cortex, developed by Eric Sabag. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Neural DSP Technologies Oy.

What the app collects

Nothing. Rigside does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Rigside itself sends nothing over the Internet; the Internet permission in the app comes from Google Play's billing library, which uses it only to check and complete Premium purchases with Google Play.

What stays on your phone

  • Your setlists, songs and app settings are stored only on your device.
  • Preset files are created only when you choose to share or back up presets, and go only where you send them.
  • Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Bluetooth

Rigside uses Bluetooth only to find and talk to your Nano Cortex. On Android 12 and later it asks for the "Nearby devices" permission and is declared as not using it to determine location. On Android 11 and earlier, Android requires the location permission for any Bluetooth scan. Rigside uses it only for that scan and never reads your location.

Purchases

Rigside Premium is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Rigside receives only Google's confirmation that the purchase exists, and does not see your payment details.

Children

Rigside is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
